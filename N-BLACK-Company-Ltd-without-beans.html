About N-BLACK

From Nigerian Farms To Global Markets

N-BLACK COMPANY LTD is an agricultural export-focused company based in Jos, Plateau State, Nigeria.

Quality Produce. Reliable Supply.

We focus on connecting buyers with agricultural products sourced from Nigeria. Our goal is to provide dependable sourcing and professional handling for local and international customers.

From grains and legumes to roots, spices and other agricultural commodities, we work toward making Nigerian produce accessible to wider markets.
